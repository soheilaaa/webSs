The problem

Most Disputes Start on Paper

Contracts get signed under tender pressure, notices sit in someone’s inbox and changes are agreed on site. The cost shows up later.

Up to£25bn
lost every year to avoidable errors in UK construction
2,264
adjudication referrals in a year, the most on record
73%
of UK subcontractors routinely face payment disputes, and 79% of them name unagreed variations as a top cause

Sources: Get It Right Initiative, research on the cost of avoidable error in UK construction. King’s College London and the Adjudication Society, construction adjudication report, November 2024. Payapps survey of UK subcontractors, September 2026.
